About

I started in graphic and web design at agencies, moved into front-end and interface work, and spent the last four years inside a fintech: first as its only designer, then as part of a two-person team that wrote product requirements, reviewed scope with our director of product and owned its own sprint.

I like data-dense tools, clear systems and working close to engineering, connecting product intent with implementation effort and constraints. I prototype end to end with AI, read and adjust front-end code when it helps, and rebuilt my team’s process around AI without letting it drift from the design system.
